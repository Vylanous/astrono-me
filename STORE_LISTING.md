### Description

Understand the week through your chart.

ASTRONO-ME is a Canadian astrology companion for people who like astrology best when it is
specific, dated, and honest about what it is. It calculates your birth chart on your
phone and follows the Moon's real movement, then reads the two together — the way a
working astrologer would — in plain, grounded language.

Every reading names the actual planet, sign, aspect, and date. When the Moon changes
sign on Thursday, your outlook says Thursday. When the Moon squares your natal Saturn,
it says that too, and explains what a square tends to feel like and what it is useful
for. Readings end with a short reflection prompt, because the point is your own
thinking, not a verdict from the sky.

WHAT IS FREE
- A daily reading based on your Sun sign and the day's actual Moon sign and phase
- Your Sun, Moon, and Rising placements (the Rising sign appears when you know your
  birth time; if you do not, ASTRONO-ME says so instead of guessing)
- A full list of your planetary placements
- The first two Learn articles

ASTROCAN PREMIUM — CA$7.99/MONTH OR CA$69.99/YEAR
- Written interpretations for every placement in your chart, Mercury through Pluto
- Your Week: the weekly outlook, built from the week's real Moon sign changes and
  quarter-phase dates, with notes where the Moon aspects your own chart
- Compatibility readings: add a second person's birth date (with their agreement) for
  a calm reading of the two charts together
- The full Learn library: houses in plain English, what a transit is, Mercury
  retrograde explained honestly, and more
- A saved-readings archive, kept on your device
- Audio versions of Your Week, in production and included when ready

The annual plan is about 27% less than paying monthly for twelve months
(12 × CA$7.99 = CA$95.88). There is no free trial — you are charged when you confirm.
Premium renews automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel in one step from your
App Store subscription settings, which the app links to directly. The free daily
reading stays free either way.

PRIVACY, PLAINLY
There is no account and no server. Your birth details, preferences, and saved readings
are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded, sold, or shared, and there is no
advertising and no tracking. You can export everything the app stores, or delete all of
it, from Profile and settings.

WHAT ASTROCAN DOES NOT DO
ASTRONO-ME does not predict the future, and its readings are not medical, financial, or
legal advice. Astrology is a symbolic language, not a tested science. ASTRONO-ME treats
it as a tool for reflection — a way to notice the week you are in — and says so in the
app, where it belongs.

Positions are calculated on-device with a simplified ephemeris, accurate to sign and
phase for reading purposes. Made in Canada.
