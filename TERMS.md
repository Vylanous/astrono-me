## What ASTRONO-ME is

ASTRONO-ME is an astrology companion for **reflection and entertainment**. It calculates
birth charts and the Moon's movement using a simplified on-device ephemeris and pairs
those positions with written interpretations. Readings describe tendencies, themes,
and timing in symbolic language.

**Readings are not predictions, and they are not advice.** ASTRONO-ME does not provide
medical, mental-health, financial, legal, or any other professional advice, and nothing
in the app should be relied on for decisions in those areas. Astrology is not a tested
science. If a decision matters, consult a qualified professional and treat the chart as,
at most, a prompt for your own thinking.

Calculated positions are accurate to sign and phase for reading purposes; they are not
intended for navigation, research, or any precision use.
