# ASTRONO-ME Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to ASTRONO-ME version 1.x_

ASTRONO-ME is an astrology companion for reflection, made in Canada. This policy explains,
in plain language, what happens to your information when you use the app. The short
version: **in this version of ASTRONO-ME, your information never leaves your device.**

## What ASTRONO-ME stores, and where

ASTRONO-ME stores the following **only on your device**, in the app's local storage:

- The birth details you enter: first name (optional), birth date, birth time (if you
  provide it), and birthplace city.
- Your preferences: reading tone (Gentle or Direct) and cadence (Daily or Weekly).
- Readings you choose to save in the archive.
- For compatibility readings, a second person's name, birth date, and birthplace — only
  if you add them.
- If you use a preview build, a record of the simulated subscription state.

There is **no ASTRONO-ME account, no ASTRONO-ME server, and no analytics or advertising**
in this version. We do not receive, see, sell, rent, or share any of this information,
because it is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Planetary positions are
calculated on your device.

## Other people's birth dates

Compatibility readings ask for a second person's birth date. A date of birth is
personal information. Only enter someone else's details if they have agreed to be
included. The second profile is stored only on your device, and you can remove it at
any time from the Compatibility screen, or wipe it with Delete All My Data.

## Your control: export and delete

- **Export my data** (Profile and settings) shows you the exact data stored on your
  device, as text you can copy. Nothing is sent anywhere when you export.
- **Delete all my data** (Profile and settings) permanently removes your birth profile,
  saved readings, preferences, and any second profile from the device and resets the
  app. Uninstalling the app also removes this data.
- Deleting app data does **not** cancel a store subscription. Subscriptions are managed
  in your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings, which the app links to
  from Profile and settings.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under those
stores' own privacy policies. ASTRONO-ME does not receive or store your payment card
details. In preview builds, purchases are simulated on the device and no payment is
taken at all; the app says so on the paywall.

## Children

ASTRONO-ME is intended for adults and is not directed at children. Birth details for a
child should only be entered by their parent or guardian, and are stored on the device
in the same way as any other profile.

## Canadian privacy law

ASTRONO-ME is developed in Canada. Because your information is stored only on your own
device under your control, the collection and disclosure obligations of Canada's
*Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA) and applicable
provincial laws have very little to attach to in this version — there is no collection
by us to consent to. If a future version introduces accounts, cloud storage, or
analytics, this policy will be updated first, the app will say what changed, and any
new collection will be opt-in where the law requires it.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new

Questions about privacy in ASTRONO-ME can be sent to the developer contact listed on the
